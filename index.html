10 Hour Hackathon for High School Students

Challenge: Build an online tool that helps others study more effectively. This could be flashcards, planners, games, or something completely new.

Location: Economic Alliance Building • 501 1st St SE, Cedar Rapids, IA
Duration: 10 hours

Who Should Join?

Any high school student who wants to build, create, and learn. Beginners and experienced coders are welcome.

  • Work solo or with a teammate
  • Compete for prizes in innovation and technical skill
  • Enjoy free food, drinks, and a comfortable workspace

Why Attend?

Sharpen your coding skills, solve real problems, and collaborate with students across different grade levels. This is also a great experience for resumes and college applications.

What to Bring

Laptop and charger

Notebook and snacks if wanted
